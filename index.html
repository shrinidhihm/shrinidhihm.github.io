Hello there!

Aerodynamics & CFD Engineer

I am a Master’s student specializing in aircraft aerodynamics and Multidisciplinary Design Optimization (MDO) , with practical experience in Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD), turbulence modeling, and simulation-driven design. I enjoy analyzing and optimizing complex systems, and I am motivated to apply theory to real-world aerospace projects through interdisciplinary collaboration, development of tools that connect research with practical aircraft design and presenting innovative solutions

I am currently pursuing my MSc in Computational Sciences in Engineering at Technische Universität Braunschweig in Germany. As part of my Master’s studies, I have worked on the aerodynamic design and optimization of high-lift systems. In addition, I have experience with eVTOL concepts and structural analysis of aircraft wings using finite element analysis.
During my Bachelor’s studies, I was deeply involved in SAE Aero Design, starting as Lead Aerodynamicist at national competitions and later serving as Team Captain. I led a 14-member team to compete internationally at Van Nuys, California, an experience that continues to drive my passion for aerospace engineering.

Sustainable aviation is what truly excites me. Small improvements in drag, lift, and overall efficiency compound over an aircraft’s operational life, translating into tangible reductions in fuel consumption and environmental impact. I am drawn to the multidisciplinary nature of aircraft design and motivated by how tightly coupled aerodynamics, structures, and systems are.

Outside of engineering, I spend my time hiking in the mountains, biking through the towns around Braunschweig, and taking long walks on forest trails.
