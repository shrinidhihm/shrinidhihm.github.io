SAE India - Aerothon 2021 - Virtual Aero Design Contest

As team captain, I directed a seven-member team through the complete UAV design process, including aerodynamics, structural analysis, and simulations for a 5.28m wingspan aircraft with 25kg MTOW targeted at disaster surveillance.

I spearheaded CAD modeling and aerodynamic optimization using SolidWorks and XFLR5, while conducting CFD and structural analyses in Ansys Workbench to refine the design.

Additionally, I drove design improvements, collaborated on technical documentation, and presented our work, focusing on innovations like solar integration for extended endurance.

Overview

SAEINDIA AEROTHON 2021 was a virtual competition challenging students to design unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) for real-world applications, emphasizing innovation in aerospace technologies.

Key technical requirements included a minimum 3-hour endurance, 6kg payload capacity, maximum 50kg weight, 150km/hr speed, and operation up to 6000m above sea level. The UAV was to support delivery of essentials to remote areas and surveillance for safety operations.

Our team designed a UAV incorporating high-lift airfoils (S1223), a hybrid wing planform (rectangular inboard sections blended with tapered outboard panels, plus winglets for enhanced aerodynamic efficiency), solar cells for extended range, and Arduino-based controls.

The process covered airfoil analysis, wing/fuselage/tail design, spar sizing, endurance calculations, and avionics selection, with innovations in sustainable power systems.
