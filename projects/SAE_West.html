SAE International - SAE Aero Design West 2022 – Van Nuys, California, USA

SAE Aero Design West 2022 was a defining milestone in my undergraduate journey. I had the opportunity to lead Team Vymanika as Captain for SAE International’s premier aero design competition, held in Van Nuys, California.

Our team designed, analyzed, fabricated, and prepared a radio-controlled aircraft under strict dimensional and performance constraints, while simultaneously managing the complexities of international travel, logistics, and competition readiness during the post-COVID period.

This experience remains one of the most transformative phases of my engineering development, combining technical depth with leadership, responsibility, and resilience.

Overview

SAE Aero Design West is an international collegiate competition organized by SAE International that challenges teams to design, build, and fly radio-controlled aircraft across three classes: Regular, Advanced, and Micro.

In 2022, Team Vymanika competed in the Micro Class, where the mission requires aircraft to launch from a small, raised platform (simulating a short takeoff run) and deliver standardized payload boxes of two different sizes — representing small and large pizza boxes — while maximizing speed on the first lap and overall score under strict geometric, weight, and power constraints.

Our design philosophy centered on achieving exceptional Short Take-Off and Landing (STOL) performance. Drawing inspiration from classic bush planes like the Piper Super Cub, we adopted a tail-dragger configuration with a steerable rear wheel and a high static angle of attack. This allowed the aircraft to rotate quickly and lift off within the limited distance available after leaving the launch table.

To further optimize weight and structural integrity, we designed and 3D-printed custom support mounts for the landing gear as well as the landing gear wheels themselves, ensuring precise fit, reduced mass, and enhanced durability tailored to the tail-dragger layout.

A critical practical requirement was transporting the aircraft from India to the USA as checked luggage. To enable safe and compliant air travel, we engineered the aircraft for easy disassembly and reassembly: the wings were made fully detachable, the tail surfaces and tail boom were removable, and the fuselage remained a robust single-piece structure. Custom protective boxes were fabricated to securely house the disassembled components during transit, allowing rapid on-site assembly upon arrival in Van Nuys, California.

An early prototype featured an integrated payload bay but proved excessively tail-heavy, resulting in longitudinal instability during testing. Through iterative refinement, we shifted to a mid-wing layout with conventional tail-dragger landing gear, significantly improving static and dynamic stability while preserving the high-lift characteristics essential for Micro Class performance.

The final aircraft successfully demonstrated reliable takeoffs within the prescribed distance, stable flight characteristics, and effective payload accommodation during pre-competition testing and on-site demonstration flights.
