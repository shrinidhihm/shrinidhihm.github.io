SAE International - SAE Aero Design West 2022 – Van Nuys, California, USA

SAE Aero Design West 2022 was a defining milestone in my undergraduate journey. I had the opportunity to lead Team Vymanika as Captain for SAE International’s premier aero design competition, held in Van Nuys, California.

Our team designed, analyzed, fabricated, and prepared a radio-controlled aircraft under strict dimensional and performance constraints, while simultaneously managing the complexities of international travel, logistics, and competition readiness during the post-COVID period.

This experience remains one of the most transformative phases of my engineering development, combining technical depth with leadership, responsibility, and resilience.

Tools & Methods

  • OpenVSP & XFLR5: Preliminary aircraft sizing and aerodynamic analysis.
  • SolidWorks: Full-scale CAD modeling and integration.
  • Ansys Fluent: CFD simulations for lift, drag, and flow behavior.
  • Ansys Static Structural: FEM-based structural validation.
  • Documentation: MS Word, MS PowerPoint, MS Excel.
  • RC Aircraft Fabrication and Assembly.
  • Technical Documentation & Presentation.
  • Flight Testing and Performance Evaluation.

Team & Leadership

I led a 12-member team, comprising 6 core team members and 6 interns. As Captain, I was responsible for coordinating all technical, managerial, and logistical aspects of the project.

  • Overall project leadership and decision-making
  • Task allocation, progress tracking, and technical reviews
  • Coordination between design, analysis, fabrication, and documentation teams
  • Primary point of contact for institutional and external coordination

Technical Scope & My Contributions

I was deeply involved in the complete aircraft development cycle, with particular responsibility for aerodynamic and structural integrity of the design.

  • Preliminary aircraft sizing and configuration development
  • Aerodynamic modeling and stability analysis using OpenVSP and XFLR5
  • CFD analysis using Ansys Fluent to assess lift, drag, and flow behavior
  • Structural analysis and validation using Ansys Static Structural
  • Full-scale CAD modeling and integration support
  • Supervision of complete aircraft fabrication and assembly

Management, Finance & Logistics

Beyond engineering, the competition demanded strong organizational execution. I oversaw critical non-technical responsibilities essential to international participation.

  • Secured institutional funding of INR 7.5 Lakhs (≈ €8,600 as of Sept 2021)
  • Coordinated and successfully managed US visa applications for 7 teammates
  • Oversaw financial planning and expenditure monitoring as part of captaincy duties
  • Managed travel logistics, accommodation bookings, and on-ground coordination in the USA

Competition Experience

The competition environment at Van Nuys demanded rapid decision-making, composure under pressure, and strong teamwork. We successfully completed all technical inspections, documentation reviews, and demonstration requirements.

Impact & Legacy

We were the first team from our institution to compete at SAE Aero Design West in the USA. This effort laid the foundation for future teams and directly supported the successful participation of the 2023 team at SAE Aero Design East in Fort Worth, Texas.

Leading this team under challenging circumstances shaped my confidence as an engineer and leader, and strongly influenced my decision to pursue advanced studies in aerodynamics and aircraft design.
