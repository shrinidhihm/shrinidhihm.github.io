Overview

The SAE Aero Design Challenge 2021 aimed to design a radio-controlled aircraft capable of lifting the maximum possible weight while complying with specified power limits and aircraft dimensions (length, width, height).

Team SWIFT 7 focused on a monoplane configuration with a rectangular wing, flaps, aluminum fuselage for strength and weight reduction, balsa wood wings, and depron tail. The project emphasized real-world unmanned air vehicle requirements, balancing design process and communication skills.

Due to COVID-19, we adapted by using improvised materials like carton and aluminum rods for the initial prototype, successfully building and test-flying it despite resource constraints.

Design Considerations

  1. Monoplane configuration with rectangular wing.
  2. Flaps for enhanced lift.
  3. Aluminum fuselage for reduced weight and increased strength.
  4. Balsa wood for wing fabrication.
  5. Depron for tail fabrication.

Fabrication and Testing

Due to the COVID-19 pandemic and associated restrictions, the physical competition was cancelled. However, the team successfully adapted by constructing and test-flying a functional prototype using readily available materials such as carton and aluminum rods to validate the core design concepts, aerodynamics, and flight characteristics.

The intended final aircraft was designed to utilize aluminum for the fuselage (for strength and weight reduction), balsa wood for the wings, and depron sheets for the tail section. Selected propulsion components included the iFlight Xing 660KV motor and a 16x10E propeller.

Despite the inability to fabricate the full-scale aircraft with the planned materials, the prototype flights provided critical validation data. The team submitted comprehensive application, design, and fabrication reports, along with a virtual technical presentation, as part of the modified competition format.

Conclusion

Final parameters after analysis and market survey:

  • Airfoil for Wing: S1223
  • Wing Configuration: Rectangular
  • Empennage Configuration: Conventional
  • Materials: Balsa, Depron, Aluminum
  • Motor: iFlight Xing 660KV
  • Propeller: 16x10E

The project demonstrated effective design, fabrication, and testing under constraints, aligning with real-world UAV requirements.
