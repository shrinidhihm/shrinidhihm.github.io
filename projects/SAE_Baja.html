SAE Baja – Team VVCE Baja (2020)

As a key member of Vidyavardhaka College of Engineering's inaugural Baja team, I spearheaded fundraising and sponsorship efforts, raising ≈ ₹50,000 (≈ €625 as of Feb/Mar 2020) in 90 days through, crowdfunding, graphic design (posters, brochures, team logo), cold emailing alumni, social media campaigns, and delivering a sales presentation at Chitkara University. I also led cost reporting, co-authored technical documentation (DFMEA, DVP&R), handled administrative tasks, and assisted in vehicle fabrication.

Overview

SAE Baja is an international collegiate design competition organized by SAE International, challenging undergraduate engineering students to conceive, design, fabricate, and compete with a rugged single-seat all-terrain vehicle (ATV) capable of enduring harsh off-road conditions while meeting strict safety, performance, and cost rules.

In India, BAJA SAEINDIA (sponsored by Mahindra in 2020) is one of the country's largest student engineering events, drawing hundreds of teams. The 2020 season included physical events at Chitkara University (Chandigarh) in early March, featuring static evaluations (design, cost, sales presentation) and dynamic events (acceleration, maneuverability, endurance).

As Vidyavardhaka College of Engineering's very first Baja team (established 2019), we faced typical new-team challenges: limited resources, tight timelines, and building everything from scratch.

My contributions focused on the business and organizational foundation, securing critical funding through rapid crowdfunding and sponsorship drives, maintaining accurate cost tracking, promoting the team, and supporting technical documentation. These efforts enabled the purchase of essential tools and equipment while establishing sustainable processes that benefited the team in 2020 and future seasons.
