SAE Baja – Team VVCE Baja (2020)

Active member of VVCE Baja, the inaugural SAE Baja team established at Vidyavardhaka College of Engineering in 2019. Played a pivotal role in fundraising, sponsorship outreach, marketing, cost reporting, administrative tasks, and design documentation for the 2020 competition season.

Spearheaded the crowdfunding and sponsorship campaign, raising approximately ₹50,000 INR (≈ €625 as of Feb/Mar 2020) in 90 days through alumni outreach, social media, posters, presentations, and direct pitches.

Part of a 25-member student team building an all-terrain vehicle (ATV) under the guidance of faculty advisors.

Overview

VVCE Baja was the first SAE Baja team from Vidyavardhaka College of Engineering, formed in 2019 with around 25 members. The team designed and built an all-terrain vehicle for the 2020 SAE Baja competition, focusing on off-road performance, durability, and innovation.

My contributions spanned critical non-technical areas—fundraising, sponsorship acquisition, marketing, financial tracking, and administrative support—while also assisting in documentation and fabrication. These efforts were essential for securing funds quickly and keeping the project organized.
